Rover — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Rover is a local-first AI agent for iPhone. This policy explains exactly what data leaves your device and where it goes.

The short version

  • Your conversations, memories, and settings are stored only on your iPhone.
  • When you send a message, the text of that conversation is sent directly to the AI provider you configure (e.g. DeepSeek, Zhipu GLM, Moonshot, Anthropic, or your own Ollama server). That is how any AI assistant works — the model must read your request to answer it.
  • The provider you choose receives your text under their privacy policy. We do not proxy, log, or copy that traffic. There is no Rover server.
  • Without an API key, Rover runs in demo mode using a built-in local engine. Depending on your prompt it may still perform real web searches or fetch public web pages you ask for.

Rover has no analytics SDKs, no advertising, no crash reporting, no tracking, and no accounts. We (the developer) receive nothing.

Data disclosure summary (App Store label)

Rover discloses: Other User Content — not linked to identity — not used for tracking — App Functionality. This reflects that conversation text is transmitted to your configured AI provider and retained by them beyond the duration of a single request. Nothing else is collected.

Permissions

Rover asks for permissions only when you use the matching feature:

  • Reminders: when you ask the agent to create a reminder.
  • Clipboard: read only when you ask it to.

Your control

  • Delete any memory fact from the Memory page with a swipe.
  • Edit or clear your profile anytime.
  • Delete the app — everything goes with it. There is nothing on our side to delete, because there is nothing on our side.
